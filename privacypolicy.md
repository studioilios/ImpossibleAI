# Impossible AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Impossible AI ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares and protects your information when you use the Impossible AI mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

## 1. Information We Collect

1. **Account information:** your name and email address, used to create and secure your account.
2. **Fitness and health information:** workouts, runs, rides, lifts, yoga sessions, nutrition entries, goals, recovery inputs, XP, levels and the Overall Health score calculated from this data.
3. **Location information:** precise location while you use the App, to show nearby quests, gyms and zones, to track runs and routes, and to calculate territory captured by your squad.
4. **User content:** squad activity, community posts, challenge participation, and messages you send to RED, the in app AI fitness coach.
5. **Device and usage information:** a user ID, device type, app version, crash logs and basic interaction data used to keep the App working and fix problems.

We do not collect your contacts, and we do not read data from Apple Health.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

1. To provide core features: XP, quests, leaderboards, squads, zone capture, run tracking and health stats.
2. To power RED, which generates workout and nutrition plans and real time feedback.
3. To keep your account secure, including one time code login.
4. To diagnose bugs and improve App performance.
5. To respond to support requests.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data for third party advertising or cross app tracking.

## 3. AI Processing

When you chat with RED, your messages and relevant fitness context (such as recent workouts, goals and schedule) are sent to Google Gemini, a third party AI service, to generate a response. You are asked for permission before your first message is sent, and you can decline. Declining disables RED but does not affect other features. We do not allow the AI provider to use your data to train its models beyond what its API terms permit for service delivery.

## 4. Information Visible to Other Users

1. Your display name, level, XP and leaderboard rank.
2. Zones and territory your squad captures, which are derived from your recorded runs.
3. Posts and challenge activity you choose to share in the community.

Your exact live location is not shown to other users.

## 5. Service Providers

We share data only with providers that help us run the App:

1. **Supabase:** account authentication, database and file storage.
2. **Google Gemini:** AI responses for RED.
3. **Map provider:** map display and location search.

These providers process data on our behalf and only for the purposes above.

## 6. Data Retention

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we must keep limited records to meet legal obligations.

## 7. Your Rights and Choices

1. **Access and correction:** view and edit your profile in the App.
2. **Account deletion:** delete your account and associated data from Settings inside the App, or by emailing us.
3. **Location:** turn off location access at any time in iOS Settings. Map and run tracking features will not work without it.
4. **AI consent:** withdraw consent for RED at any time in the App settings.

If you are in India, you have rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you have rights under the GDPR, including access, correction, deletion, restriction and portability. Contact us to exercise any of these rights.

## 8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS and stored with access controls. No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

## 9. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 10. Medical Disclaimer

Impossible AI is for fitness tracking and general information only. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. We will change the "Last updated" date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the App.
